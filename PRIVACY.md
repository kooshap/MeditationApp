# Privacy Policy — Meditate

*Last updated: November 2026*

## The short version

Meditate does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Ever.

## The full version

**Data collection:** None. The app makes no network requests and has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or third-party SDKs. Because no data is collected, none is shared with third parties.

**Data stored on your device:** Your timer preferences (duration, selected bell, volume) and the dates you meditated (used to show your streak) are saved locally using iOS system storage (`UserDefaults`). Only the date is kept, not the time or length of a session. This data never leaves your device.

**Links:** Tapping "Privacy Policy" in the app opens this page in your web browser. That page is hosted on GitHub, and the visit is governed by [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), not by the app.

**Permissions requested:** None. The app does not send notifications and requests no device permissions.

## Data retention and deletion

Your timer preferences and meditation dates stay on your device only for as long as the app is installed. Deleting the app permanently removes them. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing to request from us.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
