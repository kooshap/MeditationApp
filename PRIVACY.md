# Privacy Policy — Meditate

*Last updated: September 2026*

## The short version

Meditate does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Ever.

## The full version

**Data collection:** None. The app makes no network requests and has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or third-party SDKs. Because no data is collected, none is shared with third parties.

**Data stored on your device:** Your timer preferences (duration, selected bells, volume) are saved locally using iOS system storage (`UserDefaults`). This data never leaves your device.

**Notifications:** The app requests permission to send local notifications solely to ring the ending bell when your session completes while the screen is locked. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on-device — no data is sent to any server.

**Links:** Tapping "Privacy Policy" in the app opens this page in your web browser. That page is hosted on GitHub, and the visit is governed by [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), not by the app.

**Permissions requested:**

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Rings the ending bell when the app is backgrounded or the screen is locked |

No other device permissions are requested or used.

## Data retention and deletion

Your timer preferences stay on your device only for as long as the app is installed. Deleting the app permanently removes them. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing to request from us.

## Withdrawing consent

You can turn notifications off at any time in **Settings → Notifications → Meditate**. The timer keeps working; the ending bell just won't ring while the screen is locked.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
